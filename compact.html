Riemann Hypothesis & Generalized Riemann Hypothesis in Lean 4

An interactive architecture map and plain-English guide to Levent Alpoge's Lean 4 formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) and Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH), built for software engineers and programmers exploring formal mathematical proofs.

About This Viewer

The Riemann Hypothesis predicts that every non-trivial zero of the Riemann zeta function lies on the critical line Re(s) = 1/2 (and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis extends this to all Dirichlet L-functions), tightly governing how prime numbers are distributed. Lean 4 is an interactive theorem prover and dependently typed programming language whose small kernel mechanically checks every step of a mathematical proof.

What this viewer does

Organizes the 794-file compact Lean 4 repository into an interactive dependency graph, top-down call stack from Main.lean, visual intuition diagrams, trust audit, and clickable math-to-engineering glossary.

Who it is for & how to explore

Designed for software engineers and technical readers without a math PhD. Start with the Prime Numbers & GRH Crash Course, inspect the Architecture Map, trace Main.lean's Call Stack, check the Trust Audit & Lean 4 Rosetta Stone, or press / to search modules.
